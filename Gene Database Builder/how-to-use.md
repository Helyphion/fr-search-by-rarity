### Updating Gene Data
1. Open `view-source:www1.flightrising.com/scrying/predict` and ctrl + F for `name="bodygene"`, `name="winggene"` or `name="tertgene"`, depending on what has been updated.
2. Copy the block of modern genes at the top of the section, or the block of ancient genes below, depending on what was updated. Paste it into the corresponding txt file in the `data` folder. Ideally remove all extra indentation (using shift + tab). Remember to remove the line for Basic in the modern block (`option value="0"`), and the `selected="selected"` part of whichever line that shows up in.
